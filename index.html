01

One rule for every sense

Visual design. Sound design. Motion design. Even taste and smell.

They all share one thing. Every element you use, every component you connect, comes with a cost. So you have to budget it carefully.

VisualSoundMotionTasteSmell

02

Two kinds of cost

The cost is not only technical. It also shapes how the human brain sees and feels the work.

Technical

The machine pays. Every effect costs performance.

Cognitive

The mind pays. Every effect costs attention.

03

More is not premium

Too many shadows. Too many effects. Everything over the top. It does not feel premium. It feels like generic slop. The noise drowns out the intent.

Noise

Everything shouts. Nothing lands.

Intent

One thing, with room to breathe.

04

Budget, don’t pile

The budget is fixed, for the machine and for the mind. Spend it on the few things that matter, so they have room to breathe.

Piled on120%

Over budget. Nothing has room.

Budgeted60%

40% left for room to breathe.

Detail is not the enemy. Unbudgeted detail is.

Nothing is free. Spend with intent

Less noise. More meaning. That is what premium feels like.
